चतुर्थी विभक्तिः: the dative case

This chapter’s grammar answers one question: to whom, or for whom?

ॐ सूर्याय नमः। Salutations to the Sun.

माता याचकाय वस्त्रं ददाति। Mother gives clothes to the beggar.

Two things always call for the dative: the word नमः (salutation) and the verb ददाति (gives).

Pick a word to see its dative forms

Words are grouped by how they end. Each group follows one rule.

The verb ददाति (to give)

Present tense (लट्) says what happens. Command (लोट्) requests or urges.

How a “giving” sentence is built

The receiver takes the dative ending. The verb usually comes last.
